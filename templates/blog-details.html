撰稿合作常见问题解答

我们致力于与专业分析师和撰稿人建立长期、互利的合作关系。以下是关于撰稿合作最常见的问题解答，希望能帮助您更好地了解我们的合作模式。

1 文章的主题和角度是由公司指定，还是我可以基于自己的专业判断自由选择？ Will the article topics and angles be assigned, or do I have the freedom to choose based on my professional judgment? [English]

我们鼓励两者结合。公司会提供一个宏观的主题方向（如"俄罗斯能源政策的东亚转向"），但我们高度尊重并依赖您的专业判断来选取具体角度和论证，这正是我们合作的价值所在。我们寻求的是您独特的、基于本地视角的洞察。

We encourage a combination of both. The company will provide broad thematic directions (e.g., "The Pivot of Russian Energy Policy towards East Asia"), but we highly respect and rely on your professional judgment to define the specific angle and argument. This unique, on-the-ground insight is the core value of our collaboration.

2 对于内容的观点和结论，公司是否会进行干预或审查？ Will the company intervene in or censor the views and conclusions presented in the content? [English]

我们承诺不干预基于事实和专业分析的独立观点。我们的编辑流程主要侧重于核实事实数据、逻辑结构和语言表达，以确保分析的严谨性和清晰度。当然，所有内容需符合专业操守和中国法律法规。

We commit to non-interference with independent views based on facts and professional analysis. Our editorial process focuses on verifying factual data, logical structure, and language clarity to ensure analytical rigor. All content must, of course, adhere to professional standards and Chinese laws and regulations.

3 稿费的支付周期和支付方式是怎样的？ What is the payment cycle and method for the remuneration? [English]

稿费在文章通过最终审核并采纳后支付。我们提供灵活的支付方式，包括银行转账和主流国际支付平台（如PayPal/Wise），支付周期通常在收到发票后的14个工作日内。

Remuneration is issued after the article passes the final review and is accepted. We offer flexible payment methods, including bank transfer and major international platforms (e.g., PayPal/Wise). The payment cycle is typically within 14 business days upon receipt of a valid invoice.

4 除了基础稿费，是否有基于文章表现（如阅读量、影响力）的额外奖金？ Beyond the base fee, is there a performance-based bonus (e.g., readership, impact)? [English]

目前我们的合作模式以单篇固定稿费为主，这能保障您的核心智力付出获得稳定回报。对于产生重大行业影响力的杰出作品，我们会进行个案奖励，并在后续合作中优先提供更高稿酬的深度项目机会。

Currently, our model is primarily a fixed fee per article, ensuring stable compensation for your intellectual input. For outstanding pieces that generate significant industry impact, we offer case-by-case bonuses and will prioritize offering more in-depth, higher-paying project opportunities in the future.

5 合作的长期性和稳定性如何？是单篇约稿，还是系列合作？ What is the longevity and stability of the collaboration? Is it a one-time engagement or a series? [English]

我们致力于建立长期、稳定的合作关系。我们更倾向于与优秀的分析师签订系列合作协议，这能保证您有持续的产出和收入，也便于我们对某一领域进行持续深入的跟踪研究。

We are committed to building long-term, stable collaborations. We prefer to establish series-based agreements with excellent analysts, which ensures consistent output and income for you and allows us to conduct sustained, in-depth research on specific areas.

6 我撰写的文章版权归属如何界定？ How is the copyright of the articles I write defined? [English]

文章一经采纳并支付稿费，其完整版权（包括复制、发行、信息网络传播等权利）将归属于本公司。您有权将该文章作为您的个人研究成果收录于您的作品集或简历中。

Upon acceptance and payment, the full copyright (including rights of reproduction, distribution, and information network dissemination) of the article belongs to the company. You retain the right to include the article in your personal portfolio or CV as a sample of your work.

7 在写作过程中，我能否获得公司的数据、研究报告或内部资料支持？ During the writing process, can I access the company's data, research reports, or internal resources for support? [English]

当然。对于签约合作者，我们会视项目需要，分享相关的公开资料、数据摘要以及我们的宏观研究框架，为您的研究和写作提供坚实的背景支持。

Absolutely. For our contracted contributors, we share relevant public materials, data summaries, and our macro-level research frameworks on a project-needs basis to provide solid background support for your research and writing.

8 公司如何保障我的个人信息和专业身份安全？ How does the company ensure the security of my personal information and professional identity? [English]

我们将严格遵守保密协议。您的个人信息和作者身份将受到严格保护，未经您的明确许可，我们绝不会向任何第三方披露。我们理解您所处领域的敏感性。

We strictly adhere to confidentiality agreements. Your personal information and author identity are protected with the utmost care and will not be disclosed to any third party without your explicit permission. We fully understand the sensitivity of your field.

9 是否有机会与公司内部的其他专家或我的同行进行交流？ Are there opportunities to interact with other experts within the company or my peers? [English]

我们定期举办线上闭门研讨会，邀请核心合作者参与。这是与公司内部专家及其他领域同行进行思想碰撞、分享见解的绝佳平台，我们鼓励您积极参与。

Yes, we regularly host online, closed-door seminars for our core contributors. These are excellent platforms for intellectual exchange with our in-house experts and your peers from other fields, and we highly encourage your participation.

10 除了写稿，我是否有可能参与更深入的合作，如联合研究、咨询项目或媒体评论？ Beyond writing, are there opportunities for deeper collaboration, such as joint research, consulting projects, or media commentary? [English]

非常有潜力。撰稿合作是建立彼此信任的第一步。对于表现卓越的专家，我们非常乐意将其推荐给我们的客户，并邀请参与高价值的联合研究、定制咨询项目，甚至代表公司出席媒体活动，这将是价值提升的关键路径。

Very high potential. Contributing articles is the first step in building mutual trust. For outstanding experts, we are delighted to recommend them to our clients and invite them to participate in high-value joint research, customized consulting projects, and even represent the company at media events. This is a key path to value enhancement.
